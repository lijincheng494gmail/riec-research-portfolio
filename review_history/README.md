# Public revision evidence

This directory provides public-safe, source-grounded records of how external
editorial and reviewer criticism changed two RIEC manuscripts.

- [`ARRAY_CHANGE_MATRIX.md`](ARRAY_CHANGE_MATRIX.md) follows RIEC-L1 from the
  first major revision through editorial return, reconstruction, minor revision
  and publication.
- [`FACTORY_CHANGE_MATRIX.md`](FACTORY_CHANGE_MATRIX.md) follows the Factory
  manuscript from major revision through a substantive R1 rebuild and final
  rejection.
- [`SOURCE_ARTIFACT_REGISTER.md`](SOURCE_ARTIFACT_REGISTER.md) records the
  source-artifact roles, sizes and SHA-256 digests used to verify this history.

## Public-disclosure boundary

The matrices paraphrase scientific criticism and revision actions. Raw decision
letters, reviewer reports, response letters and editorial-system bundles are
not uploaded because they contain private contact details, reviewer or editor
information, manuscript identifiers and tracking links. A checksum proves which
author-held artifact grounded a statement without redistributing confidential
correspondence.

This record does not imply that rejection validates a method, that acceptance
endorses later RIEC projects, or that every reviewer request was scientifically
necessary. It documents what was challenged, what changed, what remained
unresolved and how the claim boundary moved.
