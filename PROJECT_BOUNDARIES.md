# Project boundaries

## RIEC-L1

Supports auditable conflict resolution for the studied finite candidate libraries and grouped engineering datasets. It does not establish a universal model-selection criterion or unrestricted external generalization.

## RIEC-BCI

Supports the conclusion that evaluation world can materially alter apparent motor-imagery EEG performance, ranking and selection in the tested six-dataset benchmark. It does not identify a universal decoder, demonstrate clinical readiness, establish neural mechanism or prove zero-shot transport.

## RIEC-Core

Defines and freezes a protocol-to-claim governance architecture. Factory, Dairy, Eco and Chemistry were development evidence; they are not post-freeze external validations. The accepted scope waiver explicitly excludes component-level necessity/effectiveness, route-by-route necessity/redundancy, complete activation coverage and causal or external-benefit claims. Neuro results cannot be used to rewrite v1.0.

## RIEC-Agent

Demonstrates a local-to-global evidential failure and a safety-discovery trade-off inside the tested synthetic multi-agent evidence market. It does not establish universal control for all agents, providers, scientific domains or real deployments. The LLM-provider study is supporting available-case evidence, not the primary proof.

## Programme-level boundary

The shared contribution is an auditable way to constrain claim authority under analytical multiplicity. The programme does not claim that a single predictive model transfers across the represented domains.
